FONTLOG for Diddo Sans
======================

This file provides detailed information on the Diddo Sans font software.
This information should be distributed along with the Diddo Sans fonts and
any derivative works.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

Diddo Sans is a single-style (Bold) display typeface for the logotypes and
promotional material of Diddo products. It reproduces the lettering of the
"diddo" and "pad" logos, which were made by converting Montserrat Regular
text to outlines and adding a thick round-joined stroke.

Diddo Sans is a Modified Version of Montserrat, released under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1 (see OFL.txt). It is NOT the original Montserrat and is not
endorsed by its authors.


Derivation
----------

Upstream: Montserrat, https://github.com/JulietaUla/Montserrat
          commit 555facfb2a18c72c3c0380f0d9c0f060453a9058, sources/Montserrat.glyphs
          (Regular instance, weight 400).

All changes are made by scripts/diddoize.py and scripts/fixes.py:

- Subset to the v1 character set (Basic Latin, Spanish, basic typographic
  punctuation).
- Skeleton changes: tapered "Diddo" stems with round ends on b d i l k a p q,
  a foot on the bowl letters, taller ascenders (857) and longer descenders
  (-331.5), single-storey a (from Montserrat's a.ss01), p and q derived from
  the d by rotation/reflection, larger and higher i/j dot, counters of o d b a g
  reduced to match the 2021 logo outlines.
- Round terminals: every free stroke end of the skeleton (stems, terminals,
  arms, bars, figures, accents) is replaced by a half-disc of the stroke
  width, without growing the glyph.
- Emboldening: union of the filled outline and a 95-unit round stroke.
- Rescaling (x 0.8805) so that the visible cap height is 700 units; side
  bearings enlarged by the stroke offset.
- Glyph fixes: dieresis, percent, at, copyright, registered, ordinals,
  raised top marks.
- New vertical metrics, names and version.
